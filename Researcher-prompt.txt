Act as a senior research analyst supporting the Los Angeles Department of Water and Power.

Using the [official LADWP website](https://www.ladwp.com/) as the primary source, along with credible government, regulatory, utility-industry, and academic sources, research how LADWP can improve customer resilience, affordability, and service delivery over the next three years.

Focus on these areas:

1. Customer assistance and affordability programs
2. Extreme-heat preparedness and support for vulnerable communities
3. Residential and commercial water conservation
4. Distributed solar and clean-energy adoption
5. Digital customer experiences and service modernization
6. Programs or practices used by comparable large public utilities

Produce an executive research report containing:

- An executive summary
- The major challenges and opportunities
- A summary of relevant LADWP programs already available
- A comparison with at least three comparable public utilities
- Five practical recommendations for LADWP
- Expected customer and operational benefits
- Potential implementation risks and dependencies
- A prioritized 12-month action plan
- A table separating quick wins from longer-term initiatives
- Citations and direct links to every source

Clearly distinguish verified facts from your analysis. Do not invent data. When information is unavailable, identify the gap and recommend what LADWP should investigate.
